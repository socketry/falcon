Concurrency and parallelism let us balance latency, throughput, and hardware efficiency.

---

These are execution strategies, not universal improvements.

Concurrency can improve utilization by doing useful work while other tasks wait. Parallelism can reduce latency or increase throughput by applying more execution resources. Both introduce coordination costs and trade-offs.
