# Title

Asynchrony

# Subtitle

Work can remain outstanding without blocking progress.

---

Asynchrony is the root capability: initiation and completion are decoupled, so an operation can remain outstanding while the initiator continues making progress.

It does not prescribe how that outstanding work executes. It may be interleaved concurrently, executed in parallel, or handled by an external system.
